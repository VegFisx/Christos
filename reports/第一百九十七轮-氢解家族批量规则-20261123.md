> 只保留模板和验证都通过的；同一批试过的季铵化/还原胺化模板未通过，
> 没有入库。

## 1 规则

### 1.1 叔溴氢解

`reduction_dehalogenation_cbr_001`

```text
D4 C-Br  + H2  ->  C-H + HBr
```

### 1.2 叔醇脱氧

`reduction_deoxygenation_tertiary_001`

```text
D4 C-OH  + H2  ->  C-H + H2O
```

两条都是：

- 只匹配 **D4 叔碳**，避免一级/二级底物；
- 模板由 2-溴-2-甲基丙烷 / 2-甲基-2-丙醇标准例派生；
- `condition_profile: reduction`；
- `provenance: cited`。

文献：
- *Hydrogenolysis of carbon-halogen bonds with Chromium(II)-EN perchlorate*,
  Org. Synth. 1972, DOI 10.15227/orgsyn.052.0062；
- *Hydrogenolysis of carbon–halogen and carbon–carbon bonds over
  Pd/Nb2O5–Al2O3 catalysts*, Catal. Commun. 2009,
  DOI 10.1016/j.catcom.2009.05.024；
- *Reductive Deoxygenation of Alcohols: Catalytic Methods Beyond
  Barton–McCombie Deoxygenation*, Eur. J. Org. Chem. 2013,
  DOI 10.1002/ejoc.201300657；
- *Radical deoxygenation of tertiary alcohols*, Tetrahedron Lett. 1982,
  DOI 10.1016/s0040-4039(00)87249-4。
