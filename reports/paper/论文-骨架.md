## Abstract（v1.0.0 数字版；每格都能在 §1 表里找到出处）

我们在公开基准 USPTO-50k 上对一套逆合成模板库做**全库逐条审计**（泄漏受控：先量化与模板来源的
重叠，再剔除 3,007 条抽取记录后重聚类——同算法在未剔除时**精确复现生产库 3,228 条**，剔除后
**3,065 条**为基准库）。主要发现：（i）假路线高度集中——56 目标审计集上，54 条模板贡献 83.8% 的
无效尝试；（ii）在 50k 上，"前体比目标大"（R2）占 57.5%，其中 top-5 模板占 52.7%（芳环 C-H
官能化的逆用）；（iii）广泛使用的"残基/相似度"型可用性判据基于**限时 MCS**，判定可能取自部分
搜索结果（超时率与三轮负载重判的翻转率如实报告）；（iv）黑名单/确定性修补在三种分布上一致
奏效（确定性判据默认开启后"假可用"由 30.78% 收紧到 **29.22%**）；（v）**截断饱和**：同一 20k 池
上 `cap8 → cap32` 使单步召回 59.40% → **75.80%**（McNemar p=4.1e-25，零回归），全量 50k 上
`cap8 → cap32` 为 70.43% → **82.56%**（泄漏版）/**82.32%**（**泄漏剔除版，本文报此值**，
Wilson 95% CI 81.98–82.65%）。逆向侧：覆盖率 **94.74%**（泄漏剔除版，n=5,002；freq≥10 门槛版
91.90%），多步端到端 **47.67%（depth≤2）/ 72.33%（depth≤3）**（n=300）。应用侧给出

### 2.1 【草稿】Template-based retrosynthesis and its engineering filters

Template-based retrosynthesis extracts reaction rules from reaction corpora (rdchiral,
Coley et al. 2019) and searches over their application (Retro*, Chen et al. 2020;
AiZynthFinder, Genheden et al. 2020). Much of the published progress is about *search* and
*ranking*: neural policies, value functions, and engineering filters that discard
chemically implausible expansions. These filters are usually introduced as implementation
defaults with a short justification; their **definitions, thresholds, and failure modes are
rarely the object of study**, and — to our knowledge — no prior work audits a template
library **template by template** on a target distribution while reporting **concentration**
(which few templates cause most bad routes), **reproducibility of the validity criterion**,
or **provenance** of every rule back to a reaction record.

### 2.2 【草稿】Benchmarks and evaluation quality

USPTO-50k (Schneider et al. 2016) is the de-facto single-step benchmark; ORD (Open Reaction
Database) supplies community reaction records with provenance. Recent work increasingly
notes that route-quality metrics are heterogeneous and hard to compare; our contribution
here is narrower and empirical: we show that a widely used *residue/similarity* validity
criterion is built on **time-boxed maximum common substructure** calls, that such calls time
out on a substantial fraction of medium-sized pairs, and that the verdicts taken from
partial searches are therefore **machine-state dependent** (reported as a negative result
plus a two-audit reproduction delta).

### 2.3 【草稿】Provenance as a first-class artifact

We additionally attach a reaction-record identifier to **every** template (100% coverage),
which turns "template quality" from a modelling question into a **governable data
question** — the same stance we then apply on the applied side with a pharmacopoeia-anchored
predicate chain (§3.4), where every ordering basis carries an official entry URL.

### 3.4 【英文稿·已并入】Pharmacopoeia-anchored, provenance-first ordering

To make the ranking basis of input compounds externally verifiable, we built a predicate
chain from the *Chinese Pharmacopoeia (2025 edition)*. Each monograph's **Assay**
(含量测定) and **Identification** (鉴别) sections were extracted, curated (OCR-shape repair,
section-label rejection, multi-component "total content" sentence reconstruction), and then
linked to our compound database; every row carries the official entry URL. The chain covers
**386/491 (78.6%)** of the pharmacopoeia entries in our scope with **3,277 linked rows
(58.0%)**; a 400-row blind re-check against source text gives **93.5% supported**
(**98.7%** after excluding 5.3% known extraction artifacts; an independent re-check with a
stricter windowed matcher agreed on all 400 rows, zero disagreements). Ordering is **tiered**:
Tier-0 (Assay, quantitative limits) precedes Tier-1 (Identification), and within a tier,
**the official limit value** defines the order — no model score is involved. The behaviour is
gated behind a switch that is **off by default**, so all headline results elsewhere in this
paper are produced without it. The chain is updateable: re-running three scripts against a
new structured pharmacopoeia release regenerates the tables; a source-watcher reports upstream
changes.

### 4.3 【英文稿·已并入】Case study on Glycyrrhiza (甘草)

Running the production configuration on 12 constituents of Glycyrrhiza with the ordering
switch enabled, the ledger contains **552 distinct candidate products (552 provenance paths)**;
the two compounds that the 2025 Pharmacopoeia quantifies for this herb —
**glycyrrhizic acid (≥2.0%)** and **liquiritin (≥0.50%)** — head the candidate list, each
carrying its limit and source entry. A second demonstration with a "total content" herb,
Andrographis (穿心莲), shows the curation layer recovering a four-component total sentence
("andrographolide, neoandrographolide, 14-deoxyandrographolide, dehydroandrographolide") into
linkable components. These cases illustrate the design stance of the system: generate
comprehensively, mark honestly (direction, condition evidence, provenance), never delete or
silently rank, and expose every ordering basis to the user. The same chain also serves a
single-herb-a-day usage pattern: after each batch the system writes a one-page HTML report
and a run ledger entry, so results remain auditable without expert supervision.

### 5.1 【英文稿·已并入】Limitations (honest)

1. **No human blind evaluation.** We could not obtain independent expert annotators; we
   substitute objective proxy criteria plus a curated gold check (93.5%/98.7% supported on
   400 rows) and report this limitation explicitly.
2. **Heuristic usability.** Route-usability verdicts rely on residue/enlargement/inert/absurd
   heuristics with bounded MCS matching; timeouts persist (documented), which may make
   borderline verdicts machine-dependent.
3. **ORD-internal evaluation.** Templates are mined from, and evaluated on, community-standard
   ORD/USPTO-derived data; we controlled evaluation leakage (75,718 leaked rows removed;
   leak-free numbers reported) but external cross-database evaluation remains future work;
   an automated-retrosynthesis baseline comparison is pending.
4. **Pharmacopoeia chain is a downstream layer, not an oracle.** It depends on a third-party
   structured version of the standard; 21% of in-scope entries are not yet linked
   (mineral/aggregate/tail categories), and total-content sentences required bespoke
   reconstruction.
5. **Conditions are marked, not simulated.** Temperature/solvent/reagent windows are annotated
   with evidence strength; we do not claim kinetic, yield, or selectivity prediction.
   The blind-annotation protocol therefore separates **three axes** — structural soundness,
   whether the required conditions are conventional, and whether the required reagents are
   commonly available (rare/expensive catalysts or ligands are flagged explicitly) — because
   *structurally feasible* is not *practically usable*; literature condition samples from ORD
   are shown to the annotator where the template has provenance.
6. **Scope discipline.** The system is a general reaction engine; herb-specific scenarios
   (e.g., decoction chemistry) are intentionally excluded from this version.
7. **Data-provenance audit (added 2026-10-16).** For the TCM-side formula corpus we ran a
   fixed-seed audit of AI-retrieved ancient prescriptions (n=300): 28.0% matched a public
   source composition-wise (Wilson 95% CI 23.2–33.3%), 66.3% matched in name only, 5.7% were
   not found in the queried channels (absence in our channels ≠ non-existence); every record
   carries a provenance tier (0 pharmacopoeia … 5 AI-retrieved) and is never hidden or deleted.

### 5.2 【草稿】Why "precursors larger than the target" dominate — a chemistry reading

On the full benchmark, cuts whose precursor set is larger than the target (class R2) account
for **57.5%** of all cuts, and **top-5 templates alone for 52.7%** of them; the family is the
**reverse of aromatic C–H functionalization** (Ar–H → Ar–F/Cl/Br/I/O/Me, i.e. using
"de-halogenation"/"de-functionalization" as a synthetic step). Chemically this is not
nonsense — late-stage C–H functionalization is a real strategy — but as a *disconnection* it
silently assumes that the C–H precursor is a cheap, available starting material. When the
engine consumes building blocks rather than designed intermediates, such "editing" steps
disguise themselves as synthesis: the model proposes to *unmake* a functional group it was
never told how to install.

Three consequences we act on:

1. **Audit, don't hand-wave.** We enumerate the family across the target distribution instead
   of trusting a support-count threshold: the first blacklist (frequent-templates, ≥5
   occurrences) **misses** this family; a second, distribution-conditioned list (B8, 29
   templates, all R2, **0 usable**, 45.8% of all cuts on 50k) had to be built explicitly. It
   ships **off by default** (3,025 → 2,942 templates when enabled), so headline numbers stay
   comparable to the literature.
2. **Availability conditions the verdict, not the deletion.** The residue-family verdict
   becomes "implausible *unless* the precursor is purchasable" (C3): a reverse-editing step is
   legitimate exactly when the alkene/halide/arene it starts from is a real reagent in the
   terminal set. This keeps the system honest without deleting candidates.
3. **Distribution-conditioned governance is the right next knob.** Because "which editing
   reactions are acceptable" depends on the target distribution (natural-product-like vs
   block-like targets), the actionable output is a *conditioned* list plus the audit script,
   not a single global threshold — and every entry of it is traceable to reaction records
   (C4).
