## 0. 主线定稿（B1）

**统一式论文**：*"A quality-audited, evidence-carrying reaction prediction system:
full-corpus template mining, truncation saturation, and pharmacopoeia-anchored ordering"*

* **主贡献**：质量审计与修复框架（黑名单/方向/可用性判据/确定性修补）＋ **全量数据规模消融** ＋
  **截断饱和发现**（"更多模板反而掉点"的机制与修复）；
* **应用章**：药典判据链（2025 版，行带官方出处）——"来源可溯的排序依据"，TCM 场景示范；
* **口径**：不打分、不排序、不删产物；所有生成项带出处与不确定标记；正向数字报**泄漏剔除版**。

候选贡献句（投稿时选一句打磨）：
> We show that, on community-standard reaction data (ORD, 1.77M reactions),
> **template-based prediction quality is limited less by data volume than by
> corpus-audit hygiene and candidate truncation**, and we provide a reproducible
> audit-and-repair framework that lifts single-step product recall from 55.4% to 82.3%.
